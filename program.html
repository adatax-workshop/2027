Format

Talks and posters

Each invited talk slot is 35 minutes: 30 for the talk and 5 for questions, with the moderator alternating between the room and the online queue. Each contributed talk is 10 minutes, 8 for the talk and 2 for questions. Every accepted paper, from both tracks, presents a poster.

Across the day, 232 minutes are reserved for discussion in one form or another: question periods, the poster session, the panel, and the lunch break.

Panel discussion

The 50-minute closing panel brings together four of the invited speakers, covering all three questions and both academic and industry perspectives, moderated by an organizer. Each panelist gives a two-minute answer to the panel question, followed by 20 minutes on the problems submitted to the open-problems board and 20 minutes of questions from the room and the online queue.

One panelist is asked to argue that the tax is overstated or an artifact of current evaluation, so that the panel does not become a consensus session.

Joining Remotely

Every part of the day can be followed and joined without being in the room.

  • Livestream of the full program from 09:00 to 17:00 with live captions, and a single moderated question queue for room and stream.
  • Virtual poster hour for remote presenters. Remote posters are also shown on a screen in the physical poster area with a QR link to the presenter's three-minute video.
  • Pre-recorded talks for speakers or authors who cannot travel for visa, health, or similar reasons, played in their slot with live remote questions.
  • Recordings of all invited and contributed talks on this website within two weeks, subject to each speaker's consent.
